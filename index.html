Our story

A Riyadh coffee house that grew out of Najdi hospitality.

Najd Coffee is a Riyadh coffee house built on Najdi hospitality, where a pot of Arabic coffee is poured for every guest who walks in.

We roast our beans in small batches so each cup tastes fresh, and we serve it the way we would in our own majlis.
