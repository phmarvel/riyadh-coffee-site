Our story

From a Najdi majlis to a cup in Riyadh.

Najd Coffee began in 2019 with one dallah and one idea: coffee is a welcome before it is a drink. We roast every week in small batches, grind with cardamom the way our grandparents did, and serve it with dates.

Every guest is poured a first cup on the house, because that is how a Najdi majlis begins.
